# NNNN. The decision, as a short statement

## Context

What made a decision necessary: the problem, the forces and constraints at
play, and what was known when it was taken.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense.

## Consequences

What follows from it, the good and the bad: what it makes easy, what it rules
out, and what has to stay true for it to hold.

## Alternatives considered

Each option weighed against it, and why it was not taken.
